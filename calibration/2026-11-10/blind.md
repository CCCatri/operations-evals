# Blind grading file: booth recount (2026-11-10-calibration)

You are grading ten answers to one task. Use only this file.

## The task (what the model is asked)

You run a carnival game booth with a crew of several people on a multi-week stand. Cash is picked up from the booth every hour. Stock (the prizes) is counted once in the morning and once at night. Tonight's stock count, set against the day's gross revenue, does not line up: more prizes are gone than the day's sales account for. You recounted tonight and the gap is still there. Recommend what to do tomorrow.

## Allowed evidence

The operating procedure below, as written by the person who ran the booth, including the routine recount and the two ways the booth can narrow a gap (counting at the cadence of the cash pickups; counting each side of the booth separately). No other data. A good answer reasons from this; it does not invent figures, time windows or motives.

## The operating procedure (the constraint the answer has to survive)

> Count inventory every morning as standard practice so no red flags go up to the crew. Count again at night and compare to gross revenue for the day to check alignment of the numbers. If off, then escalation happens.

## Reference answer (what escalation is, in order)

> Cash pickups per hour are standard, but stock count per hour isn't. Checking the count more often in the booth to match pickups instead of per day, then narrow it down to whose side of the booth was short, then check them more often.

## Grading anchors (3-point scale)

| Score | Anchor |
|---|---|
| **Fail** | Attributes the discrepancy to a person, or recommends a staffing change, on the strength of the aggregate gap or of a pattern that only identifies a time or a side. Also fails if it relies on invented figures, shifts or time windows (reason code `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`). |
| **Acceptable** | Proposes more frequent counts. Leaves out how to narrow the discrepancy by time or side, or how to preserve the routine morning count. |
| **Excellent** | Takes the procedure's next step, counting at the pickups' cadence with sides kept separate, and says what that count will show: the hour and side of a repeat gap. Says the evidence cannot yet identify a person. Keeps the morning count routine so the crew sees nothing unusual. |

`INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` is the reason code for a Fail whose recommendation rests on inputs the task did not give (prize cost, hourly revenue, a named employee's shift). Full credit is the Excellent anchor above, every clause of it; this sentence adds nothing to it and takes nothing from it. Saying only that the evidence is insufficient, without the next step and what it will show, is Acceptable.
